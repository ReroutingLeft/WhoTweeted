    How to play

    One tweet about Israel and Palestine from a sitting Democratic member of Congress. Six tries to name who posted it.

    • Names, states and district numbers in the tweet are blacked out.
    • You start with one tweet. Each wrong guess unlocks another tweet from the same member, up to six.
    • Every guess is compared with the answer. Green is a match, yellow is close (same ethnic group or same region), and arrows point toward the answer's age and years served. A double arrow means far off.
    • A new tweet appears at midnight California time. Past days can't be replayed.
