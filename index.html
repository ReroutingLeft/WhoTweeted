    A new puzzle every day at midnight California time. Posts from X (2021–2023, via the Tweets of Congress archive) and Bluesky (2024–today).

    How to play

    Posts about Israel and Palestine from a sitting Democratic member of Congress, on X or Bluesky. Six tries to name who posted them.

    • Names, states and district numbers in the tweet are blacked out.
    • You start with one post. Each wrong guess unlocks another post from the same member, up to six. The most-liked posts come last.
    • Every guess is compared with the answer. Green is a match, yellow is close (same ethnic group or same region), and arrows point toward the answer's age and years served. A double arrow means far off.
    • A new tweet appears at midnight California time. Past days can't be replayed.
